## Kitchens, Baths, and the Projects Homeowners Ask About Most

Kitchen work is the most common call we get, whether the house is in Southeast Portland or a newer build out near Salmon Creek. [Kitchen remodeling](/kitchen-remodeling-portland) usually starts with a conversation about layout. Older homes on both sides of the river tend to have closed-off kitchens that don't fit how people actually cook and gather anymore, so a lot of the work is about opening things up, replacing cabinetry, and bringing in countertops that can handle daily use for the next twenty years.

Bathrooms are a close second. [Bathroom remodeling](/bathrooms-tile) projects range from a full gut-and-rebuild to something more targeted, like swapping out a dated tub for a walk-in shower with custom tile. Vancouver has a good mix of older ranch-style homes and newer construction, and the bathroom needs are different for each. An older home might need the plumbing and subfloor addressed before the pretty finishes go in, while a newer house might just need a layout that makes better use of the square footage.

## ADUs, Additions, and Basements for Growing Households

Accessory dwelling units have become a popular answer for families who want rental income, space for aging parents, or a place for adult kids to land without everyone sharing one roof. Our [ADU and home addition](/adu-home-additions-portland) work covers detached units, garage conversions, and additions that extend the existing footprint of a house. Washington and Oregon have slightly different permitting rules, so if you're in Vancouver it's worth asking us early about what that process looks like for your specific lot and jurisdiction.

Basements are another underused resource in a lot of older homes. [Basement finishing](/basements) turns a cold storage space into an actual living area, whether that's a guest suite, a home office, or just more room to spread out. A lot of Pacific Northwest basements deal with moisture at some point, so part of doing this work right is making sure the space is properly sealed and insulated before anyone starts picking out flooring.
